The Seven Questions of Acoustic Analysis

Purpose: When detecting and analyzing a sonar contact, acoustic analysts systematically answer these seven fundamental questions to build a complete tactical picture.

Framework: Based on the IWADC mnemonic, extended with Speed and Action assessment.

Remember: IWADC + SA

I-W-A-D-C-S-A
Question Analysis Focus
1Identification What type of contact is this? (Submarine, surface vessel, biological, noise, etc.)
2Where Where is the contact located? (Bearing, range, depth if available)
3Aspect What is the contact's orientation relative to own ship? (Bow-on, beam, stern, etc.)
4Distance How far away is the contact? (Range estimation using various methods)
5Course What direction is the contact traveling? (True bearing of movement)
6Speed How fast is the contact moving? (Knots, blade rate analysis)
7Action What is the recommended tactical response? (Track, classify, evade, engage, report)

Application: These questions should be answered in sequence for every sonar contact. The first five (IWADC) establish the contact's identity and geometry. Speed (S) provides motion analysis. Action (A) determines the tactical response based on all previous answers.
